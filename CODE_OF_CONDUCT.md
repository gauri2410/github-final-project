# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming,
respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, insults, personal or
political attacks, publishing another person's private information without
permission, and any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate
in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to
behavior they consider unacceptable, and will remove, edit, or reject
contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual is representing the
project or its community in public spaces. Representation includes using an
official project account or acting as an appointed representative at an online
or offline event.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers through the contact
method published in the repository. Reports will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of the
person reporting an incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may apply these measures based on the nature and impact of a
violation:

1. **Correction:** A private or public response explaining why the behavior is
   inappropriate and requesting that it stop.
2. **Warning:** A warning, with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary restriction:** A temporary restriction from project
   interactions.
4. **Permanent restriction:** A permanent ban from project spaces.

Maintainers will consider the circumstances and impact of each incident when
choosing a response.
